How the picks are made

Each game is simulated thousands of times from every receiver's route share, target rate, depth of target, catch rate and yards per catch, both teams' tempo, the total and spread, the opponent's pass defense, venue, kickoff window and forecast. Teammates share the game's pass volume, so paired legs are priced together. A leg makes the list only when the model's number beats the price by a clear margin, the player's own record supports it, and a plain-language reason survives. Games a teammate missed count at 30%, games under a different quarterback at 50%, deep rungs are excluded, and players on a new team are shown but never picked.
