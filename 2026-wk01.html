How the picks are made

A simulation of each game, thousands of times, built from each player's route share, target rate, depth of target, catch rate and yards per catch, both teams' tempo, the total and spread, the opponent's pass defense, venue, kickoff window and forecast. Teammates share the game's pass volume, so paired legs are priced together. A leg makes the list only when the model's number beats the price by a clear margin, the player's own record supports it, and a plain-language reason survives.
